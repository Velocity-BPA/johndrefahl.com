About

I am the founder and technical lead of BIDLINE, a voice-to-bid platform for trade contractors, and I am currently available for senior or principal employment with a company that has real operational complexity to build for or automate. Most recently I was Founding Engineer at Octane, where I built an AI-powered back office integration platform for fuel retailers from zero.

BIDLINE is the most complete example of what I build. A plumber, electrician or HVAC tech describes a job out loud on their phone, and moments later the customer has a priced, branded quote they can review, sign and accept. Every price on that quote comes from the contractor's own price book, never from the AI. I designed and built all of it myself: a web application for the business owner, iOS and Android apps for the field, a Super Admin console for running the platform, and the AWS backend, n8n workflows, AI pipeline and operational monitoring underneath. BIDLINE runs under Velocity BPA LLC, the company I use to hold my projects and consulting work.

The node development pipeline I built has published 162 n8n community nodes to npm under the @velocity-bpa org scope. Nine custom MCP servers connect Claude AI directly to Airtable, n8n, GitHub, AWS, Notion, Stripe, npm, Dropbox, and a Claude Agents orchestration layer. I also use n8n as a full CI/CD orchestrator: the 45-node pipeline coordinates parallel Claude AI code generation, GitHub repo creation, automated testing, and npm publication end to end.

Before automation I spent 30-plus years in software development, starting in 1996 at the dawn of the web at companies including AbbVie, CVS/Aetna, Pandora, Samsung, and Apple. I have been building in blockchain and crypto since 2013. Certified in n8n at Level 1 and Level 2. I evaluated Zapier and Make before committing to n8n, specifically because enterprise organizations require the ability to self-host and control their own data environments, and I understood that from three and a half years inside AbbVie.

BIDLINE: Voice-to-Bid Platform for Trade Contractors

2026 - Present  |  Founder and Technical Lead  |  bidlineapp.com

Contractors lose jobs because quoting happens at night, after the work. BIDLINE turns a spoken job description into a priced quote the customer can sign the same day. One person designed and built the whole platform: three product surfaces on one API, with the infrastructure, AI pipeline and operations behind them.

Web Application app.bidlineapp.com  |  React, Vite, TypeScript

  • Back office for the business owner: bids, jobs with a fleet map, calendar, customers, invoices, follow-ups, seasonal campaigns and an intake leads inbox
  • A price book with import, markup rules and distributors, plus supplier-invoice capture with line-by-line review that commits received stock to an inventory and cost ledger
  • Owner analytics: win rate and pipeline, catalogue and realized margin, cash and receivables aging, crew performance and estimate accuracy
  • Workspaces with Owner, Admin, Manager and Worker roles, capability-gated screens, and subscription billing through Stripe

Mobile Apps iOS and Android  |  React Native, Expo

  • Built for the truck: a center voice button records the job, AssemblyAI transcribes it, and the priced bid comes back to the phone ready to review and send
  • Jobs, calendar and customers in the field, and on-site customer signatures captured full screen, with a signed PDF copy of every acceptance
  • One person can run their own business and work on someone else's crew from the same login, so a worker can grow into an owner on the same app
  • Preparing for release on Google Play and the Apple App Store

Super Administration Platform console  |  role-gated

  • A 360 view of every contractor with bids and customers, users and invitations, billing queues, Stripe Connect accounts and card payments, and a read-only view-as mode for support
  • Compliance tooling: an audit log with reasons and diffs, a consent log with CSV export, and an account erasure queue
  • Runtime control without a deploy: platform settings, feature flags, and an editor for the AI prompt templates with controlled activation
  • A Diagnostics console covering services, database, workflows, alarms, usage and cost, including measured AI cost per bid (about two cents)
  • AI pipeline: Claude generates the bid from the transcript, Voyage AI embeddings and Qdrant retrieve matching items from the contractor's own price book, and the platform applies authoritative prices so the model can never invent one
  • Eight production n8n workflows run customer intake, voice-to-bid, PDF generation, bid sending, notifications, bid indexing, PDF reconciliation and supplier-invoice extraction, with every call to the API HMAC-signed and the payload contracts checked in CI
  • Built to be operated by one person: separate staging and production lanes, a synthetic prober that drives every workflow on a schedule, 78 CloudWatch alarms held to a register in CI, and an n8n drift gate that fails the build when live workflows and the repository disagree
Stack: TypeScript, Node.js, Express on AWS ECS Fargate, Aurora PostgreSQL Serverless v2, DynamoDB, Valkey, Cognito, Lambda, S3, CloudFront, SES, SNS, n8n (self-hosted), Claude AI, AssemblyAI, Voyage AI, Qdrant, React, Vite, React Native, Expo, Stripe Billing and Connect, GitHub Actions
